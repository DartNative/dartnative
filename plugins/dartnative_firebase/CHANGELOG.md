## 1.0.2

- On Android, a background message that arrives while the app is running is
  now handled inside the app.
- On Android, notification taps and the answer to the notification
  permission prompt now reach your app without any code in its activity.
- On Android, each notification tap is delivered only once, even after the
  app restarts.

## 1.0.1

- On Android, the plugin no longer prints log lines when the app starts.

## 1.0.0

- First release: Crashlytics crash reports and Cloud Messaging push
  notifications from Firebase, on iOS and Android.
