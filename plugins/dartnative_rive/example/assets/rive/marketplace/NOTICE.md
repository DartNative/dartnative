## dragon_ball.riv

DartNative's own, not a Marketplace file: written by
example/tool/dragon_ball/build.sh, without the Rive editor, from a trace of
an image generated with Craiyon
(https://www.craiyon.com/fr/image/ngkUyMYoSyKO_gBOI_zQ6Q). The file keeps the
image's top 870 rows and so leaves out Craiyon's logo, which sits in its
bottom right corner; Craiyon's terms accept a credit in text beside the image
instead, and the Dragon Ball screen shows one under the artwork.

It embeds dragonball_sound_4s.mp3, the file beside it, which build.sh reads:
dragonball_sound.mp3, also beside it, from its fourth second on (build.sh
names the ffmpeg command). The sound is "Anime Powerup (Dragonball becoming
Super Saiyan) - Sound Effect for editing", by Sound Library (2023), as its
own tags name it. The screen credits it too.
